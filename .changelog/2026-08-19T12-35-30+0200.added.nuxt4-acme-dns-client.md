Migrated the working ACME DNS client-storage manager into the stack’s Nuxt 4 app with a clearer operator UI.

The old PrimeVue table dumped credentials and called acme-dns from the browser. The new client keeps register, CNAME validation, save, and delete, proxies acme-dns through Nitro so Docker `http://acmedns-server` works, masks secrets until reveal/copy, and is built from `build/acmedns-client` instead of the old Hub image.
