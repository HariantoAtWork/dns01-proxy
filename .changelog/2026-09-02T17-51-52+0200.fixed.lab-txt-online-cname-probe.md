# Fix lab TXT online hang on CNAME chains

Lab really publishes TXT via the same `updateAcmeDnsTxt` path as Certificates. The hang at **TXT online** was the authoritative probe retrying for up to 3 minutes when a challenge hop had stale TXT records but also a CNAME to the auth zone — the probe treated that as a mismatch and never followed the chain.

Probe logic now follows CNAME when present (even if stale TXT exists at the hop). Lab activity logs publish and DNS probe lines with `source: lab` so the Lab page shows what is happening during TXT online.
